Back-to-School Campaign Ecosystem.

A high-complexity seasonal program spanning multiple audience groups, timing windows, landing pages, email touches, resources, QA, and stakeholder coordination.

See the system →Back to case studies

The visible email was only one layer. The real work was coordinating the moving parts underneath it.

The challenge

Make a large seasonal campaign feel coordinated instead of chaotic.

Back-to-School required more than one audience and one send. The campaign needed timing logic, reusable assets, consistent landing-page experiences, stakeholder alignment, and a QA path strong enough to support repeated launches.

My role

Treat the campaign as an ecosystem.

01

Audience grouping

Organize the seasonal program around distinct customer groups and timing windows rather than forcing one message onto everyone.

02

Email layer

Build and coordinate the campaign touches while maintaining consistency across offers, timing, creative, and calls to action.

03

Landing-page layer

Support multiple destination versions while keeping the customer journey aligned with the email promise.

04

QA + fixes

Validate links, content, assets, audience logic, timing, rendering, and campaign readiness before launch.

05

Stakeholder path

Translate changing requests and dependencies into an organized launch process teams could follow.

What this proves

I can manage campaign complexity without losing the customer journey.

◎

Segmentation

Audience structure and timing were part of the campaign architecture, not an afterthought.

↻

Repeatability

Reusable structures help future launches move faster and reduce unnecessary rebuilding.

✓

Launch discipline

QA protects both customer experience and operational credibility when the campaign has many moving pieces.

↔

Stakeholder translation

The job is partly technical and partly human: clarify what people need, then make the system support it.

The takeaway

The best example of my campaign work is not a single email. It is the ability to hold audience, timing, assets, landing pages, QA, and stakeholder dependencies together as one launchable system.
