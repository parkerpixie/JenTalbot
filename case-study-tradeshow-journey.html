case study 03 • field marketing + sales alignment

Tradeshow to Follow-Up Journey.

A connected event campaign that treats pre-show targeting, onsite activation, post-show communication, offers, and sales follow-up as one customer journey.

See the sequence →Back to case studies

A booth is a moment. The marketing system should make that moment continue after the event ends.

The challenge

Keep the event from becoming an isolated marketing island.

Tradeshow support works best when the attendee experience begins before the show, gives people a reason to engage onsite, and continues with relevant follow-up afterward. My portfolio includes event sequences with pre-registration, reminders, booth activation, confirmation, post-show offers, and campaign paths that support sales conversations.

Pre-showtargeting, registration, reminders, offer framing, and booth awareness
Onsitebooth activation, giveaways, QR or landing-page paths, and clear next actions
Post-showfollow-up communication, savings or offer paths, and continued engagement
Salescampaign continuity that gives representatives context for the next conversation
Verticalevent messaging adapted to audience and industry needs
Reusableevent patterns that can flex across multiple shows without rebuilding the strategy from scratch

Sequence logic

Build the show as a sequence, not a date on the calendar.

01

Pre-register

Create a useful reason to engage before arrival and connect the campaign to a clear show-specific action.

02

Remind

Reinforce booth details, deadlines, offers, and the value of making time for the interaction.

03

Activate onsite

Use the booth, giveaway, registration path, or campaign destination to make participation simple.

04

Confirm + continue

Use confirmation messaging to orient the attendee and introduce the next relevant offer or path.

05

Follow up

Keep momentum after the event with communication that supports both the buyer journey and the sales conversation.

What this proves

I connect marketing moments to revenue conversations.

↗

Campaign continuity

The message, offer, CTA, and follow-up should feel connected across every event touchpoint.

◎

Audience context

Vertical and event-specific messaging helps a generic booth experience become relevant to the people attending.

↔

Sales alignment

The strongest post-show campaign gives Sales a better starting point for follow-up rather than creating a separate marketing universe.

↻

Reusable pattern

Event page + offer + pre-show email + onsite activation + post-show follow-up can become a repeatable campaign model.

The takeaway

Field marketing becomes more valuable when the event is treated as one stage in a longer journey. The work is not only getting someone to the booth. It is making the next interaction easier and more meaningful.
