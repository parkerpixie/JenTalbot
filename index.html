How AI changed my ceiling

AI lets me build the thing, not just recommend it.

I use AI as a technical collaborator, analyst, debugger, research partner, and rapid prototyping tool. It helps me cross boundaries that traditionally lived in separate roles, from campaign architecture and CRM logic to HTML, JavaScript, APIs, responsive interfaces, data structures, and working applications.

The advantage is not knowing how to prompt AI. It is knowing enough about the problem to recognize whether the answer is actually good, then turning that answer into something useful.
