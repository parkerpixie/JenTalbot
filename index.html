How I work

The system behind the send.

The visible asset is only part of the job. The real value is the repeatable machine underneath it: the audience logic, QA steps, build standards, campaign tracking, documentation, and reporting questions.

1

Request

Clarify audience, goal, offer, timing, stakeholders, assets, and what success should look like.

2

Audience

Translate targeting into segmentation, suppression, dynamic lists, and CRM-safe logic.

3

Build

Create or update emails, landing pages, forms, redirects, CTAs, and reusable content structures.

4

QA

Check links, personalization, rendering, forms, tracking, terms, mobile behavior, and launch readiness.

5

Launch

Coordinate approvals, schedule sends, monitor launches, and keep stakeholders aligned.

6

Report

Review engagement, landing page activity, campaign behavior, and the story behind the numbers.

7

Improve

Document what worked, fix what dragged, and make the next launch cleaner than the last one.

About Jen

A psychology brain, a systems habit, and a suspicious amount of patience for broken workflows.

I came into marketing automation through years of sales operations, CRM work, reporting, campaign execution, and learning whatever tool the job required next. My strength is finding the friction quickly, understanding why it matters, and bringing forward a practical path to fix it.

  • I learn tools quickly because I focus on the logic underneath them.
  • I care about the customer journey and the internal workflow that makes it possible.
  • I like clean data, repeatable processes, clear QA, and marketing systems people can actually use.
  • I believe AI only becomes useful when the inputs, structure, and governance behind it are ready.
