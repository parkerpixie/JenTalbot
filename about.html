My story

My career has moved through administrative operations, commercial marketing operations, CRM reporting, sales operations, marketing automation, campaign execution, and systems improvement. That path matters because I understand the real-world mess underneath polished deliverables.

How I work

A psychology brain, a systems habit, and a low tolerance for avoidable campaign chaos.

I am not interested in building work that only looks good in a screenshot. I care whether it is clear, usable, trackable, testable, and repeatable. The pretty front-end matters, but so does the machine behind it.

I have taught myself tools because the work required it. Salesforce, Pardot, Account Engagement, HubSpot, Dynamics, reporting tools, HTML troubleshooting, landing page fixes, documentation, and workflow cleanup have all been part of my learn-it-while-building-it career path.

  • I learn systems quickly because I look for the logic underneath the interface.
  • I ask practical questions that make the work easier to launch and easier to maintain.
  • I care about clean data because bad data quietly poisons personalization, reporting, and automation.
  • I believe strong campaigns need both customer empathy and operational discipline.
  • I like working with collaborative teams that value clarity, ownership, curiosity, and continuous improvement.
