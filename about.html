About Jen

Systems thinker. Creative utility player. Workflow translator.

When I earned my degree in psychology, I did not have a perfectly mapped career plan. I just knew I was curious about how people think, what shapes behavior, and why humans make the choices they make. That curiosity never really left. It simply wandered into CRM systems, campaign workflows, sales processes, customer journeys, and the strange little places where people and technology keep misunderstanding each other.

I first stumbled into marketing operations at TDS, working with reporting, data integrity, lead distribution, and Oracle-based CRM systems. That work taught me that clean data is never just a technical issue. It affects how teams prioritize, how leaders make decisions, and how customers move through the business.

At Accuray, I moved further into CRM and marketing campaign work, helping roll out and train teams on Salesforce in a marketing capacity while supporting campaign execution, events, customer programs, reporting, and Pardot. After a company-wide layoff, I pivoted into sales operations and dug even deeper into Salesforce CRM, large data sets, forecasting, sales process, and the full lifecycle sales journey.

Then came LGC Biosearch Technologies, right as COVID changed everything. I had three job offers land within 24 hours, accepted the role at LGC, and jumped headfirst into marketing automation during one of the most intense periods most of us have ever lived through. The work supported outreach to universities, hospitals, research teams, and other organizations involved in the urgent push around PCR testing. It was fast-paced, high-stakes, and full of systems I had to learn quickly. I taught myself the tools, asked the questions, broke a few things, fixed a lot more, and kept going.

From there, I moved into a growth opportunity at The Alliance, where I worked across Microsoft Dynamics CRM, sales operations, member data, stakeholder communications, documentation, reporting, and process improvement. When that path stopped matching the kind of work I wanted to keep building toward, I returned to marketing automation at Lands’ End, working in Salesforce Account Engagement/Pardot and pulling together everything I had learned across industries, systems, teams, and customer journeys.

The through-line is simple: I learn by doing, I ask the hard questions, and I do not treat roadblocks as stop signs. I like building the thing, understanding the system under the thing, documenting the pattern, and making the next version cleaner. I am creative, practical, curious, and stubborn in the most useful way.

  • I learn tools quickly because I focus on the logic underneath them.
  • I connect customer behavior to the internal workflows that make better experiences possible.
  • I care about clean data, repeatable processes, clear QA, and systems people can actually use.
  • I believe AI becomes useful when the inputs, structure, governance, and human judgment behind it are ready.
